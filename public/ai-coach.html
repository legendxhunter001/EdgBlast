Give me a quick breakdown of how I should think about market structure shifts on lower timeframes. Keep it tight.

2 min ago

On lower timeframes, structure shifts fastest and gets noisiest — three things actually matter:

  • Break of structure (BOS) — price closes beyond the last swing point in trend direction. Continuation signal.
  • Change of character (CHoCH) — first break against the prevailing trend. Early reversal warning, not confirmation.
  • Liquidity sweep first — lower timeframes love faking a break to grab stops before the real move. Wait for a clean close, not a wick.

A simple filter that keeps me from overreacting to noise:

pseudocode 
if close beyond swing AND volume > avg(20):
    valid_structure_break = true
else:
    treat as liquidity grab, wait

Higher timeframe structure should always outrank what you see intraday — it tells you which shifts are worth reacting to.
